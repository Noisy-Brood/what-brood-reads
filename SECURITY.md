# Reporting a security problem

If you have found a way to reach something you should not be able to, or a way
round what this code promises, report it privately: from this repository's
**Security and quality** tab, with **Report a vulnerability**, or by writing to
**security@noisybrood.com**. Either reaches a person, not a queue. Never in an
issue, which anyone can read.

Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you believe it lets
someone do. We acknowledge within three business days, keep you informed until it
is resolved, and credit you when it is fixed if you would like to be named. There
is no bounty.

## What is in scope

The code in this repository, and the service it runs in. What matters most:
anything that would let someone read another person's mailbox, obtain or use a
person's permission to it, or read what the code here promises it never reads.

## What is not

- Services we use but do not run, such as the mail provider itself. Please report
  those to them.
- Social engineering or phishing of anyone who works on Brood, and anything
  physical.
- Denial of service, load testing, and automated scanning at volume.
- Reports from a scanner with no demonstrated way to use what it found.

## Testing in good faith

We will not pursue legal action against research that stays within these lines:

- Use **your own account and your own mailbox.** Do not access, change or keep
  anyone else's data. If you reach someone else's by accident, stop, and tell us
  what you saw rather than keeping it.
- **Stop at the proof.** Show that something is possible without taking it
  further, and without degrading the service for anyone else.
- **Give us time to fix it** before you describe it publicly, and we will agree a
  date with you rather than leave it open.
